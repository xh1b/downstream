# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Critique claims, code, and evidence clearly;
allow people to ask questions, correct mistakes, and disagree constructively.
Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and
publication of private information are unacceptable in project spaces.
Avoid personal attacks and repeated disruption of a discussion.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions,
or restrict participation. They will consider context, explain decisions when
appropriate, and protect the privacy of people reporting problems.
Report concerns privately to a maintainer using the contact options on their
GitHub profile. Do not post another person's private information in an issue.
