# V2 source review — 2026-09-09

## Decision

V2 remains correctly **blocked**. This review found relevant external work but
not a source pair that satisfies the pre-registered event-specific bridge and
outcome definitions. No event scorecard or parameter was added.

## BRAC

Hooker and Knetter's NBER Working Paper 6941 is the appropriate independent
county-level closure study. It describes county employment and personal-income
effects from 1971–94 base closures and reports mostly sub-one employment
multipliers. It is relevant to the registered local-service/jobs stream, but
the public abstract/page does not supply a transcribable estimate with the
required BRAC county/window bridge, standard error, and outcome definition.
It does not provide the registered mortality or divorce outcomes. The existing
GAO Appendix II inventory is therefore retained only as a base-job exposure
inventory, not promoted to a causal score.

Primary source: NBER Working Paper 6941, *Measuring the Economic Effects of
Military Base Closures* (Hooker & Knetter, 1999; published 2001),
https://www.nber.org/papers/w6941.

## NAFTA

Hakobyan and McLaren identify effects of NAFTA tariff reductions on labor
market outcomes, including wage growth. The exposure is a tariff/local-industry
composition construct; it is not a published displaced-worker count. A tariff
unit cannot be converted into displaced workers without an additional,
independently sourced bridge. The V2 protocol forbids borrowing the outcome
estimate to construct that bridge.

Useful source locator: NBER conference manuscript, *Looking for Local
Labor-Market Effects of the NAFTA*,
https://conference.nber.org/confer/2011/ITIs11/McLaren_Hakobyan.pdf.

## Auto crisis

The review found descriptive recession/job-loss material, but no
event-specific county-level quasi-experimental estimate for the registered
mortality, divorce, or local-service outcome that cleanly separates the auto
crisis from the broader Great Recession. The national job-loss total is not an
acceptable county displacement bridge.

## Required next evidence

For a real V2 score, each event needs both files, with provenance and units:

1. an event-, geography-, and window-aligned displaced-worker point and band;
2. an independently estimated outcome point and standard error for one of the
   frozen scored streams, using the same geography and follow-up window.

The synthetic V2 fixtures test the machinery only. They are not evidence and
must never change an event from blocked to scored.
