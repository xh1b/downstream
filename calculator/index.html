Method · sources

N scales the reference calculation. N does not identify unique displaced people.

D₀ is baseline deaths. D₁ applies the displacement response. ΔD is their difference.

The baseline rate stays fixed. The calculation does not age the cohort.

Each ΔD range changes one source input. Other inputs stay fixed. The range excludes exposure, baseline, transport and process uncertainty.

Edits define assumptions. They do not establish target applicability.

The child source includes boys aged 10–14 at father firm closure. Adult earnings are observed at ages 25–33.

The lifetime conversion assumes a constant earnings gap. Negative ΔE means higher earnings.
